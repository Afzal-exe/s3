# 📦 Sample Files

Files you can upload during the labs if you don't have your own. All of them are original and made for this workshop, and the people and marks in them are fictional.

| Folder | Used in | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| [`cloud-drive/`](cloud-drive/) | [Lab 3](../lab-03-private-cloud-drive/README.md) | `Documents/` (PDF, TXT, CSV) · `Images/` (JPG, PNG) · `Videos/` (MP4) · `Backups/` (ZIP) |
| [`assignments/`](assignments/) | [Lab 4](../lab-04-assignment-submission/README.md) | Submissions by subject, plus `resubmission/` (an updated version) and `to-clean-up/` (a duplicate and an outdated file) |
| [`gallery/`](gallery/) | [Lab 5](../lab-05-image-gallery/README.md) | `Nature/` · `Events/` · `Travel/` · `Family/` illustrations. `Nature/beach-sunset.jpg` is misplaced on purpose, and `Events/blurry-shot.jpg` is meant to be deleted. |

**How to get them onto your PC:** on the repository's home page, click **`<> Code` → Download ZIP**, then extract it.

> [!TIP]
> Your own photos make the Capstone gallery far more fun. Campus, food, trips, friends: go for it! 📸
